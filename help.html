Impact Stories

2022 • Child Support Initiative

Anzio’s Story

Omega Pride Events had the honour of supporting Anzio, a 13-year-old boy who spent much of his life in and out of the Red Cross Children’s Hospital. Despite his challenges, his dream was simple — to own a cellphone.

Through a live event fundraiser, we raised enough funds to purchase him a Samsung A21. His joy was unforgettable and became a reminder of how small acts of kindness can create lasting impact.

Sadly, Anzio passed away later in 2022, but his story continues to inspire everything we do.

2024 • Animal Welfare

Majestic Beast Rescue

In December 2024, we supported Majestic Beast Rescue in Klapmuts, founded by canine behaviourist Armand Groenewald. The sanctuary has rescued and rehabilitated over 186 dogs across South Africa since 2019.

The fundraiser helped sustain food, training, and care for abandoned and abused dogs, ensuring they receive a second chance at life.

Community Development

2HO (To Help Others)

2HO began in 2019 as a local initiative and has grown into a global community of over 1,300 members. Focused on Cape Town communities like Richwood and Bothasig, the initiative supports feeding schemes and outreach programs.

Omega Pride Events helped facilitate fundraising that provided essential groceries to community kitchens and feeding programs.

2020 • Homeless Support

Haven Night Shelter

In 2020, Omega Pride Events partnered with Ms. Sheild Jacobs to raise funds for the Haven Night Shelter, a Cape Town institution supporting the homeless since 1978.

Over R4,000 was raised to restock essential food supplies. The Haven continues to provide shelter, rehabilitation, and dignity restoration programs.
